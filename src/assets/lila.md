## Senior Software Engineer, September 2025 - Present

Scientific Superintelligence, to solve humanity's biggest problems in science.

I'm currently working on our Lab Orchestration Engineering team, building the
control and management layer for our automated lab. The tools I'm building
support human scientific discovery, as well as real-world, ground-truth
feedback for our SSI. I work across the software and robotics organizations, as
well as with our scientists and engineers, to translate both human and AI
scientific thought into automated procedures.

I also support software practices across the robotics organization. I work
closely with our instrument driver team to support best practices, and help
maintain the CI/CD pipeline for the lab. My most impactful work has been at the
boundary of these two teams, providing the interface by which a diverse range
of instruments ties into the Lab-as-Software platform.
